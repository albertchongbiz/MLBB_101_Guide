Background & History

How Mobile Legends (MLBB) Began

Launched in 2016 by Moonton, MLBB was built to bring the deep strategy of PC multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) games directly to smartphones. Because smartphones are widely accessible across Southeast Asia, it exploded into a cultural phenomenon in Malaysia, Indonesia, and the Philippines.

🎮 From Casual Play to Global Pro Careers What started as a mobile game evolved into structured leagues like the MPL (Mobile Legends Professional League) and international world championships (like M8 in Turkey), transforming talented young players into professional athletes with salaries, bootcamps, and global careers.

The Roadmap to the World Stage

1
Regional Leagues (MPL MY / ID / PH) Local seasonal battles where teams fight for national glory and prize pools.
2
International Majors & M-Series (M8 Turkey) The pinnacle of esports. Top regional teams fly to global arenas representing their country.

Parent FAQ & Realities

Is professional esports a stable career?

Yes! Modern pro teams provide formal contracts, salaries, housing (bootcamps), coaching staff, physical wellness training, and media training.

What do players do during training?

It is rigorous like any professional sport: 8+ hours daily of strategic scrimmages, draft analysis, physical exercise, and team communication reviews.
