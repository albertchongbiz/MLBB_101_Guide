MLBB 101 — Core Concepts

🛡 Roles (6)

  • Tank — initiate, soak damage, CC (usually Roam)
  • Fighter — durable damage, often EXP lane
  • Assassin — burst, high mobility (often Jungle)
  • Mage — magic damage & control (usually Mid)
  • Marksman — physical DPS, scales hard (Gold lane)
  • Support — heal / shield / utility (Roam)

📍 Positions (5)

  • Gold Lane — Marksman + often Roamer early. Bonus gold from siege minions.
  • EXP Lane — Solo Fighter / Tank. Bonus experience. Near first Turtle.
  • Mid Lane — Mage. Shortest path, fast rotate to both sides.
  • Jungle — Assassin / Fighter with Retribution. Farms camps + objectives.
  • Roam — Tank / Support. No lane farm; moves with fights, vision, protect.

🐢 Key Objectives

  • Turtle — Spawns ~2:00 near EXP side. Team gold + EXP. Stops after ~8 min.
  • Lord — Spawns ~8:00. Pushes a lane hard for the team that kills it.
  • Purple Buff — CDR + mana/energy. Great for mages & skill-heavy heroes.
  • Orange (Red) Buff — Extra damage + slow on basic attacks. Preferred by ADCs & junglers.

🏰 Turrets

Each side has 9 turrets (3 per lane: Outer → Inner → Base) + the base Crystal.

  • Outer turrets fall first → opens jungle & river.
  • Destroying turrets gives gold (especially valuable in Golden Turret map variants).
  • Base Crystal destruction = victory.

⏱ Power Timing (from cheat sheet)

  • Early (0–8 min) — Can pressure early, look for kills/objectives.
  • Mid (8–15 min) — Strong around core items / levels 8–12.
  • Late (15+ min) — Scales into teamfight monster after full items.

Many heroes are “Mid → Late”. Knowing when an ultimate becomes scary helps you respect it in fights.

👀 Spectator Tip

When watching MPL or ranked, focus on:

  • Who has ultimate ready (the visual cues in the cheat sheet).
  • Who is near Turtle / Lord first.
  • Gold difference on the Gold laner — if far ahead, protect them.
  • Roam position — good roamers create picks before objectives.
